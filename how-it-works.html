How Rotational Savings Work

Save as a group. Receive your turn. Repeat the cycle.

Join a Group

Members agree on the contribution amount, schedule, and payout order.

Contribute Regularly

Every member contributes the agreed amount at each scheduled interval.

Receive Your Turn

The pooled contributions go to the member scheduled to receive that round's payout.

Continued the Cycle

Members keep contributing until everyone receives their scheduled payout.

Example

Five members contribute ₦10,000 each per month. The group collects ₦50,000 per month, which goes to the member scheduled to receive that month's payout.
